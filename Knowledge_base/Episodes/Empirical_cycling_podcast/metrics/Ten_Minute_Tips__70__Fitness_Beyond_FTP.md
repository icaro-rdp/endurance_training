# Fitness Beyond FTP — Complete Guide
_Source: Empirical Cycling Podcast — Kolie Moore, Alex Lawson, Erica Zaveta (Ten Minute Tips #70)_

---

## What Is "Fitness Beyond FTP"?

Functional Threshold Power (FTP) is a critical anchor for endurance training zones, but obsessing solely over raw FTP creates a monometric blind spot. As athletes develop, FTP gains eventually encounter diminishing returns. 

Furthermore, mass-start cycling races are rarely won by steady-state time trial power; they are decided by **event-specific physiological capacities**:
1. **Repeatable Anaerobic Work Capacity ($W'$ / FRC)**
2. **Late-Race Durability (Power output after 2,000+ kJ expended)**
3. **Neuromuscular Rate of Force Development (RFD) & High-Speed Sprinting**
4. **Submaximal Muscular Endurance (TTE extension)**
5. **Technical Execution & Aerodynamic Racecraft**

## Key Physiological Mechanisms Across Disciplines

```
              [ Aerobic Engine Baseline: FTP / VO2max ]
                                 |
     +---------------------------+---------------------------+
     |                           |                           |
[ Road / Stage ]          [ Criterium ]              [ XC / CX / Dirt ]
- Late-Race Durability    - FRC / W' Repeatability   - Explosive Start Kinetics
- Hard Attack -> Settle   - Micro-burst recovery     - High-Torque / Low-Cadence
- Climb-over-tempo blocks - High-speed flat sprint   - Handling "in the red"
```

### 1. Anaerobic Work Capacity ($W'$) & Repeatability
- High-intensity surges (e.g., covering an attack, accelerating out of a corner) draw heavily on phosphocreatine (PCr) and anaerobic glycolysis.
- The limiting factor in repeated surges is not just the absolute size of $W'$, but the **aerobic reconstitution rate of PCr** and intracellular buffering during brief rest periods ($15\text{--}30\text{ seconds}$).
- Athletes with identical 300 W FTPs will experience drastically different outcomes if Rider A can repeat $450\text{ W}$ bursts 20 times while Rider B fatigues after 8 bursts.

### 2. Durability & Muscle Fiber Recruitment Shifts
- As glycogen depletes and slow-twitch (Type I) motor units fatigue over 3–5 hours, the central nervous system must recruit less efficient Type IIa and IIx fibers to maintain the same power.
- Training specific high-power efforts *at the end* of long endurance rides trains neural motor recruitment pathways under severe substrate and metabolite strain.

### 3. Torque, Cadence, and Off-Road Neuromechanics
- Off-road starts and steep technical climbs require high angular force production at low cadences ($40\text{--}60\text{ rpm}$).
- In addition, navigating technical rock gardens, roots, and off-camber grass turns while operating near $VO_2max$ requires high sensorimotor coordination despite central brain hypoxia and high blood lactate.

## Practical Application & Workout Prescriptions

### 1. Road Racing Prescriptions
*Goal: Bridge gaps, cover attacks, and produce peak power at the end of long races.*
- **Breakaway Simulation (Attack $\to$ Settle):**
  - **Structure:** 3 minutes maximal hard start ($110\text{--}120\%\text{ FTP}$) immediately into 7 minutes at Sweet Spot / Upper Tempo ($85\text{--}90\%\text{ FTP}$). Repeat $3\text{--}4\text{ sets}$.
- **Late-Ride Repeatability:**
  - **Structure:** 3.5 hours Zone 2 endurance, immediately followed in hour 4 by $10 \times 30\text{ seconds}$ all-out sprint with $4\text{--}5\text{ minutes}$ recovery.

### 2. Criterium Prescriptions
*Goal: Anaerobic capacity, micro-recovery, and pack-speed finishing kicks.*
- **Micro-Interval Density Blocks ($30/30\text{s}$ or $40/20\text{s}$):**
  - **Structure:** $2\text{--}3\text{ sets} \times 10\text{--}15\text{ minutes}$ of $[30\text{s ON @ 130--140% FTP} / 30\text{s OFF @ 50% FTP}]$.
  - **Progression:** Start at $2 \times 8\text{ min}$; progress total time-in-set to $3 \times 12\text{--}15\text{ min}$.
- **High-Speed Sprinting from Pace:**
  - **Structure:** Sprint drills initiated on flat ground at $25\text{--}30+\text{ mph}$ (simulating pack speed) with fast, continuous upshifts.

### 3. Cyclocross & XC Mountain Bike Prescriptions
*Goal: Explosive hole-shots, low-cadence torque, and handling under extreme hypoxia.*
- **Hole-Shot $\to$ Hot Lap Drill:**
  - **Structure:** Standing start sprint (15s all-out) immediately settling into a 5-minute threshold/VO2 hot lap on technical dirt/grass. Rest 5 minutes; repeat 4–6 times.
- **Hypoxic Technical Laps:**
  - **Structure:** 10 minutes at steady Threshold on the road/trainer, immediately transitioning into technical trail descent/cornering while heart rate is at maximum.

### 4. Gravel & Ultra-Endurance Prescriptions
*Goal: Muscular endurance, TTE expansion, and gastrointestinal resilience.*
- **Extensive Tempo Progression:**
  - **Structure:** $1 \times 90\text{--}120\text{ minutes}$ continuous at $78\text{--}83\%\text{ FTP}$ within a 4–5 hour ride.
- **Consecutive-Day Volume Stacking:**
  - **Structure:** 3 consecutive days of 4–6 hour rides at easy-to-moderate endurance pace to train metabolic efficiency and recovery logistics.

## Common Pitfalls & Limitations

1. **The Pure FTP Trap:** Believing that raising FTP by 5 watts will solve poor positioning, lack of sprint power, or an inability to surge out of corners.
2. **Training Strengths Exclusively:** Repeatedly doing favorite steady-state workouts while avoiding high-variability surges or technical handling drills.
3. **Poor Energy Management on Race Simulation Rides:** Failing to fuel high-carbohydrate ($90\text{--}120\text{ g/hr}$) during long rides with end-efforts, causing workout failure due to glycogen depletion rather than fitness limits.
4. **Neglecting Neuromuscular Coordination:** Sprinting exclusively up steep hills at low speeds, failing to develop the fast leg-speed and rapid shifting mechanics required in flat bunch sprints.

## Summary Checklist / Decision Table

| Discipline | Key Non-FTP Limiters | Diagnostic Test / Indicator | Target Workout Prescription |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Road Race** | Late-race climb power; covering breakaways. | Fading on final climbs after $>2,500\text{ kJ}$. | 4 hr ride with $2 \times 20\text{ min}$ threshold in the final hour. |
| **Criterium** | Corner acceleration repeatability; sprint kick. | Getting dropped 25 mins in after 30 corner surges. | $3 \times 12\text{ min}$ of $30/30\text{s}$ or $40/20\text{s}$ at $135\%\text{ FTP}$. |
| **XC / CX** | Hole-shot power; technical handling while hypoxic. | Losing positions on first lap; crashing when redlined. | $15\text{s}$ sprint start into 5-min technical trail hot lap. |
| **Ultra / Gravel** | Muscular endurance (TTE); fueling & gut tolerance. | Extreme fatigue/cramping at hour 6; GI distress. | $2\text{ hr}$ continuous tempo interval; consecutive 5-hr weekend rides. |
| **Universal** | Maximal neuromuscular power & RFD. | Sub-1,000 W peak sprint in competitive bunch. | Heavy gym squats/deadlifts ($3\text{--}5\text{ reps}$) + flat max sprints. |

> [!TIP]
> **Coaching Gap Analysis:** When race results stall, stop asking how to raise FTP. Identify the exact moment you lost the race (e.g., dropped on a 2-minute surge, couldn't corner at speed, or ran out of gas at hour 4) and train that specific physiological and skill demand.
