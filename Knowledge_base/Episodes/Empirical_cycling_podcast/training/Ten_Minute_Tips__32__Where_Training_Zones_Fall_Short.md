# Ten Minute Tips #32: Where Training Zones Fall Short — Complete Guide
_Source: Empirical Cycling Podcast — Kolie Moore & Rory Porteous (Ten Minute Tips #32)_

---

## What Is the "Training Zone" Fallacy?

In endurance cycling, training zones (such as Coggan 7-zone power levels, 5-zone HR models, or WKO5 iLevels) are widely utilized to structure workouts. However, treating mathematical percentages of Functional Threshold Power (FTP) as absolute biological targets creates serious training errors:
* **Descriptive vs. Prescriptive Confusion:** Zones were originally conceived by Dr. Andrew Coggan as **descriptive tools** to categorize retrospective power distributions, not as rigid prescriptive recipes for workout prescription.
* **The Quantum Step Illusion:** The human body does not switch biological mechanisms when power moves from 75% to 76% FTP. Cellular signaling operates across a continuous gradient of motor unit recruitment and energy flux.

---

## Key Physiological Mechanisms / How to Think About It

```
Why Rigid Zone Prescriptions Break Down:
├── 1. The Missing Duration Dimension:
│   └── 60 seconds at 115% FTP is inside the "VO2max zone" on paper, but produces zero VO2max stimulus.
├── 2. Supra-FTP Inter-Individual Divergence:
│   └── Athletes with massive anaerobic work capacity ($W'$) require 140–160% FTP for VO2max intervals; diesel time trialists hit VO2max at 110–115% FTP.
├── 3. The Continuum of Mitochondrial Biogenesis:
│   └── Citrate synthase, Cytochrome C, and PGC-1α upregulation scale with motor unit recruitment and contraction duration, not arbitrary zone lines.
└── 4. Heart Rate Decoupling & Physiological Drift:
    └── Thermal strain, cardiac drift, hydration status, and autonomic fatigue decouple HR from actual muscle metabolic load.
```

### 1. The Continuum of Muscular Adaptation
Seminal exercise physiology studies (e.g., Dudley et al., 1982) demonstrated that mitochondrial enzyme activity (cytochrome c, succinate dehydrogenase) increases as a function of **both contraction intensity and contraction duration**:
* Lower intensity requires long durations to recruit and adapt oxidative slow-twitch (Type I) fibers.
* Higher intensity (Threshold/Sweet Spot) recruits Type IIa fibers more rapidly, inducing strong metabolic signals ($AMPK$, $CaMK$) in shorter timeframes.
* Crucially, the stimulus is governed by **accumulated contractile work under specific metabolic states**, not by hitting an exact percentage bracket.

### 2. The Flaws of Supra-FTP Percentages and Algorithmic Targets
* **Fixed % FTP Models:** Prescribing $5 \times 3\text{ min}$ at $120\%$ FTP is impossible for an aerobic diesel and trivial for a sprint-dominant rider.
* **Algorithmic Models (e.g., WKO5 iLevels / Optimized Intervals):** While customized to an athlete's modeled Power Duration (PD) curve, models rely entirely on historical data ("garbage in, garbage out"). A single breakthrough power spike or unmodeled training fatigue severely distorts algorithmic targets.

## Practical Application & Prescriptions

### 1. The Simplified 3-Zone Functional Framework
Instead of obsessing over 7 rigid power bands, structure training around a **functional fatigue-management framework**:

```
The 3-Zone Functional Framework:
├── Zone 1: Sub-LT1 Endurance (Base / Aerobic Recovery)
│   ├── Target: Below Aerobic Threshold (55–70% FTP / RPE 2–3/10 / Ventilatory Talk Test passed).
│   └── Goal: Accumulate high training volume and central stroke volume adaptations with MINIMAL accumulated fatigue.
├── Zone 2: LT1 to FTP (Threshold & Sweet Spot)
│   ├── Target: 88–98% FTP (Paced conservatively, e.g., FTP - 10W or 90% FTP).
│   └── Goal: Ride to targeted muscular fatigue; achieve progressive overload by EXTENDING DURATION (TTE progression: 3x15 -> 3x20 -> 2x30 -> 1x60 min).
└── Zone 3: Supra-FTP (VO2max, Anaerobic Capacity, Sprints)
    ├── Target: Duration-based Maximal Repeatable Effort ("Send It" / RPE 9–10/10).
    └── Goal: Achieve full cardio-respiratory engagement ($>90–95\% \text{VO}_2\text{max}$) or full contractile fatigue through all-out execution.
```

### 2. Threshold Interval Progression Rules
* **Target the Low End:** Ride threshold intervals at **FTP minus 5–10 Watts** (or 90–93% FTP for Sweet Spot). This keeps the effort below the critical tipping point where fatigue accumulates exponentially, allowing 50–100% more total time-in-zone ($TIZ$).
* **Progress Duration, Not Watts:** Never attempt to increase interval wattage from week to week while keeping duration static at $2 \times 20\text{ min}$. Hold wattage steady and progress duration: $3 \times 15\text{ min} \rightarrow 3 \times 20\text{ min} \rightarrow 2 \times 30\text{ min} \rightarrow 1 \times 60\text{ min}$.

### 3. Prescription of Supra-FTP / VO2max Work
* Prescribe intervals by **Time and Intent**, not power: "Perform $5 \times 4\text{ minutes}$ as maximal repeatable efforts, spinning at high cadence ($>100\text{ RPM}$)."
* Pacing is learned through execution: Interval 1 must be near-maximal but repeatable; intervals 4 and 5 must push the athlete into extreme respiratory distress regardless of where power ends up.

## Common Pitfalls & Limitations

> [!WARNING]
> **The "Top of the Zone" Syndrome:** Athletes obsessively hugging the upper border of every zone (e.g., riding 75% FTP on every base ride or 105% on threshold intervals) accumulate massive hidden fatigue. Conservative pacing yields superior long-term adaptation.

1. **Relying on Erg Mode for VO2max Intervals:** Clamping power in Erg mode prevents the high initial power spike needed to rapidly oxygenate muscle and achieve true $\dot{V}O_2max$.
2. **Treating Tempo as a Magic Zone:** Riding unstructured Zone 3 (Tempo) for 2–3 hours creates substantial glycogen drain without reaching the specific muscular fatigue of threshold or the low-fatigue volume benefits of Zone 2.
3. **Heart Rate Rigidity:** Trying to force power to match a predetermined heart rate zone ignores cardiac drift, room temperature, dehydration, and central nervous system fatigue.

## Summary Checklist / Decision Table

| Metric / Zone Dilemma | Theoretical Assumption | Practical Coaching Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Zone Prescriptions** | Each zone triggers a unique, distinct physiological adaptation. | Cellular adaptation is a continuum; manage workouts based on intended fatigue and duration. |
| **VO2max Power** | VO2max intervals should be prescribed at a fixed $120\%$ FTP. | Prescribe time duration (e.g. 3–5 min) and execute as maximal repeatable efforts ("Send it"). |
| **Threshold Progression** | Progress by increasing target wattage each week ($2 \times 20\text{ min}$ at $+5\text{W}$). | Lock in target wattage (FTP - 10W) and progress total duration ($45 \rightarrow 60 \rightarrow 75\text{ min}$). |
| **Endurance Rides** | Must ride at the absolute top of Zone 2 ($75\%$ FTP) to maximize base. | Ride at 55–65% FTP / comfortable conversation pace; accumulate volume without fatigue. |
| **Heart Rate Zones** | Threshold heart rate is a fixed, unchanging mathematical line. | Heart rate drifts upward over time (decoupling); use RPE and power to govern interval pace. |
