# Ten Minute Tips #45: The Simplest Training Plan Thought Experiment — Complete Guide
_Source: Empirical Cycling Podcast — Kolie Moore & Rory Porteous (Ten Minute Tips #45)_

---

## What Is the "Simplest Training Plan" Thought Experiment?

The **Simplest Training Plan Thought Experiment** investigates a core coaching dilemma: *What is the minimal, most universal training framework that will reliably drive sustainable physiological adaptation for the largest percentage of cyclists ($\sim 80\%$) across multiple disciplines?*

* **The Static 12-Week Model (Novice/Beginner):** A structured, pre-planned linear progression (Sweet Spot Base $\to$ VO2max $\to$ Race Readiness) that establishes routine, manages progression increments, and removes decision fatigue.
* **The Autoregulated Rule-Based Model (Intermediate/Advanced):** A continuous, flexible architecture consisting of fixed recovery anchors, targeted stimulus days with explicit progression rules, and autoregulated volume.
* **The Universal Truth:** Regardless of complexity, all effective training models converge on identical physiological fundamentals: **progressive overload, strict recovery discipline, and high aerobic base volume.**

---

## Key Physiological Mechanisms / How to Think About It

### 1. The Two Levers of Progressive Overload
To continually drive structural and enzymatic adaptations without inducing overtraining:
* **Sub-Threshold / Threshold Adaptations (State Function):** Stimulated by total accumulated time under oxidative stress. The primary mechanism of overload is extending **Time-in-Zone (TIZ)** at a fixed sub-threshold intensity ($\sim 88\text{--}100\%\text{ FTP}$), expanding mitochondrial density and fractional utilization.
* **Maximal Aerobic / VO2max Adaptations (Ceiling Expansion):** Stimulated by maximal cardiac stroke volume and maximal oxygen uptake kinetics ($VO_2$). The primary mechanism of overload is maximizing **Average Power / Stroke Volume** across $15\text{--}20\text{ minutes}$ of total high-cadence interval work, expanding the absolute cardiovascular ceiling.

```
Progressive Overload Modalities:
├── Threshold / Sweet Spot:  Fix Power (90–100% FTP)  -->  Progress Duration (40m -> 60m -> 80m TIZ)
└── VO2max Intervals:        Fix Duration (15–20m TIZ) -->  Progress Power & High Cadence Drive
```

### 2. Autonomic Recovery Kinetics & The "11th Commandment"
* High-intensity interval bouts and extensive threshold blocks trigger sympathetic nervous system dominance, deplete glycogen stores, and induce muscular microtrauma.
* Adaptation occurs exclusively during parasympathetic-dominant recovery windows. Violating recovery days (riding at $65\text{--}75\%\text{ FTP}$ instead of $<50\%$) preserves chronic inflammatory signaling, elevates resting cortisol, and blunts mTOR and PGC-1$\alpha$ downstream adaptations.

## Practical Application & Prescriptions

### 1. The Universal Weekly Architecture (The 2+2+1 Model)

```
The Universal Microcycle Blueprint:
├── 2x Recovery Days:
│   ├── Monday: Total Rest (0 TSS) or 30-min Coffee Spin (<10 TSS).
│   └── Friday: Total Rest or Active Recovery (<50% FTP).
├── 2x Priority Overload Days:
│   ├── Wednesday: Threshold / Sweet Spot Progression Day (Extensive TIZ focus).
│   └── Saturday / Sunday (Day 1): VO2max / High-Cadence Aerobic Ceiling Day.
├── 1x Optional / Fun / Specific Day:
│   └── Weekend (Day 2): Fast Group Ride, Skills/Trail Day, or Race-Specific Surges.
└── 2x Aerobic Base Days:
    └── Remaining time: Strict Zone 2 Endurance riding ($55–70% FTP, conversational).
```

### 2. Progression Rules for Priority Workout Days

#### A. Threshold / Sweet Spot Day (Time Extension)
* **Goal:** Extend continuous time at $90\text{--}100\%\text{ FTP}$ until reaching individual TTE.
* **Progression Scheme:**
  $$\text{Session 1: } 3 \times 12\text{ min (36 min TIZ)} \longrightarrow \text{Session 2: } 3 \times 15\text{ min (45 min TIZ)} \longrightarrow \text{Session 3: } 3 \times 20\text{ min (60 min TIZ)}$$
* **Rule:** If the athlete cannot complete the planned duration at target power without exceeding an $8.5/10\text{ RPE}$, abort the session; systemic fatigue requires an extra recovery day.

#### B. VO2max Day (Maximal Aerobic Power)
* **Target Volume:** $12\text{ to }20\text{ minutes}$ of total work duration (e.g., $4\text{ to }5 \times 4\text{ min}$ or $4 \times 5\text{ min}$).
* **Cadence Directive:** High cadence ($>100\text{--}110\text{ RPM}$) to relieve peripheral muscular torque and maximize central cardiovascular stroke volume.
* **Progression Scheme:** Increase average power across all intervals while maintaining maximal hyperventilation throughout the final 2 minutes of each repetition.

### 3. The Diagnostic Deload & Opener Protocol
Every 3 to 4 weeks of structured overload, schedule a dedicated recovery week:

```
Standard Recovery Week Structure:
├── Monday–Tuesday: Off or short easy spins (<10 TSS).
├── Wednesday–Thursday: Short Zone 2 endurance (45–60 min).
├── Friday: Active recovery or Rest.
├── Saturday (Diagnostic Openers): 45-min spin including 3x 10s max sprints + 1x 3-min sub-threshold effort.
└── Sunday: Unstructured endurance ride according to feel.
```

## Common Pitfalls & Limitations

> [!WARNING]
> **The Group Ride Substitution Error:** Athletes frequently substitute unstructured, highly stochastic group rides for structured threshold or VO2max sessions. While group rides provide excellent psychological engagement and surge durability, an uncurbed weekend hammerfest that pushes into exhaustion ruins mid-week interval execution.

1. **Riding Recovery Days at "Endurance" Pace:** Recording 40–50 TSS on a recovery day by riding at 65–70% FTP prevents parasympathetic rebound and impairs muscle glycogen supercompensation.
2. **Treating Canned Plans as Unbreakable Contracts:** Failing to autoregulate when experiencing poor sleep, illness, or high occupational stress; attempting to complete high-intensity intervals while exhausted leads to non-functional overreaching.
3. **Progressing Power Instead of Duration on Threshold:** Forcing threshold intervals at $105\%\text{ FTP}$ turns an extensive aerobic session into an unsustainable anaerobic capacity failure test.

## Summary Checklist / Decision Table

| Decision Node | Athlete Context | Prescribed Action | Execution Constraint |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Recovery Day** | Any training status | Total rest or 30-min spin | TSS must be single-digit ($<10\text{ TSS}$); Power $<50\%\text{ FTP}$ |
| **Threshold Progression** | TTE Extension Focus | Add $2.5\text{--}5\text{ min}$ per interval | Power locked at $90\text{--}100\%\text{ FTP}$; RPE $\le 8/10$ |
| **VO2max Overload** | Ceiling Expansion Focus | $4\text{--}5 \times 4\text{ min}$ all-out | Cadence $>100\text{ RPM}$; maximal respiratory distress |
| **Fatigue / Failed Rep** | Power drops $>10\%$ early | Abort session immediately | Spin easy home (Zone 1); insert extra rest day |
| **Group Ride Integration** | Morale & Race Prep | Swap for VO2max or 2nd hard day | Choose rides with high finish rate (avoid getting dropped) |
| **Post-Deload Test** | Saturday of Rest Week | 3x 10s sprints + 3m @ 90% | Assess leg feel and neural snap before next block |
