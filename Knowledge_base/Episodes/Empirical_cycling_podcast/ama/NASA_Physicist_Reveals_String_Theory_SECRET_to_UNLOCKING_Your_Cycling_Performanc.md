# Physics Models, Falsifiability & First Principles in Cycling Science — Complete Guide
_Source: Empirical Cycling Podcast — Kolie Moore & Kyle Hanson_

---

## What Is Physics Modeling vs. Biological Reality in Cycling?

In endurance sports science and coaching, there is a recurring temptation to treat human physiology as a deterministic, mechanical system governed by clean mathematical formulas analogous to classical physics. Coaches, software developers, and athletes frequently build intricate models—ranging from power-duration curves, W' reconstitution formulas, and TSS algorithms to proprietary metabolic combustion calculators.

However, a fundamental epistemological divide exists between **theoretical physics / pure mathematics** and **biological systems**:

1. **Theoretical & Mathematical Abstractions:** Mathematics provides powerful tools (e.g., Riemannian geometry, differential equations, string theory frameworks) that can operate entirely as internally consistent abstractions without necessarily mapping to physical reality unless validated by unique, falsifiable empirical observations.
2. **Biological Reality & Complex Systems:** Human physiology is a messy, highly redundant, non-linear biological network. Living organisms respond to energetic flux, cellular homeostatic disruption, autonomic stress, and hormonal milieu—not to idealized equations.

Understanding the boundaries between mathematical modeling and physiological first principles prevents athletes from falling into the trap of pseudo-scientific "hacks", over-fitted power algorithms, and non-falsifiable coaching claims.

### 1. The Principle of Falsifiability in Exercise Science

In theoretical physics, a model only graduates to an accepted scientific theory when it produces **unique, falsifiable predictions** that can be tested against empirical data (such as Dirac predicting the existence of the positron from relativistic quantum wave equations, or general relativity predicting gravitational lensing).

In cycling science, many popular concepts fail the test of falsifiability:
- **"Metabolic Hacks" & Proprietary Algorithms:** Claims that specific micro-cadences, isolated supplement protocols, or complex multi-variable power formulas magically unlock 50 watts operate as non-falsifiable marketing.
- **Model Overfitting:** Forcing an athlete's power profile into a rigid mathematical curve (e.g., a two-parameter Critical Power model) often ignores real-world biological limiters (such as muscular fatigue, fueling status, cadence-dependent motor unit recruitment, and autonomic exhaustion).

### 2. Reductionism vs. Complex Biological Signaling

In physics, fundamental forces (gravity, electromagnetism, strong and weak nuclear interactions) can be isolated at specific energy regimes (e.g., using particle accelerators like the LHC to probe quantum chromodynamics and the Higgs field). 

In exercise physiology, reductionism frequently leads to catastrophic training errors:
- **Isolated Molecular Targets:** Attempting to optimize a single signaling protein (e.g., AMPK or PGC-1$\alpha$) by chronically depriving the body of carbohydrates or performing continuous all-out intervals ignores the systemic autonomic, endocrine, and immunological breakdown that accompanies such stress.
- **The Monolithic "Science" Fallacy:** A single mechanistic in vitro study or isolated animal trial does not supersede the broad consensus of whole-body human endurance physiology.

### 3. First Principles of Energetics and Mechanical Work

Cycling performance fundamentally adheres to physical conservation laws, but biological efficiency dictates the outcome:
- **Mechanical Energy vs. Metabolic Input:** Total mechanical work ($kJ$) produced at the pedals represents ~20–25% gross metabolic efficiency; the remaining ~75–80% is dissipated as heat.
- **Substrate Kinetics:** Energy production relies on the oxidative phosphorylation of lipids and carbohydrates, alongside substrate-level phosphorylation (phosphocreatine and glycolysis). No theoretical framework can bypass the biological rate limits of mitochondrial flux, capillary oxygen delivery, or cardiac stroke volume.

## Practical Application & Prescriptions

To avoid getting lost in mathematical abstractions and pseudo-scientific trends, coaches and athletes should structure training around robust, empirical first principles:

### 1. Ground Training in Observable, Reproducible Metrics

| Metric Type | High-Yield Empirical Markers | Low-Yield / Over-Abstracted Traps |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Threshold & Capacity** | Time-to-Exhaustion (TTE) tests (30–60+ min), repeatable interval power, baseline step tests | Automated 1-minute ramp tests, estimated FTP from 20-min x 0.95 without verification |
| **Internal Strain** | Rate of Perceived Exertion (RPE), heart rate recovery kinetics, ventilatory talk test | Rigid reliance on arbitrary algorithm-derived TSS scores or readiness rings |
| **Progressive Overload** | Increasing continuous Time-in-Zone (TiZ), increasing weekly sustainable kJ | Chasing high Normalized Power spikes through stochastic surges |

### 2. Rules for Evaluating New Training Theories & Tech

1. **Ask for the Biological Mechanism:** Does the proposed method have a plausible, well-established mechanism in human cardiovascular or neuromuscular physiology?
2. **Demand Falsifiable Outcomes:** What specific, measurable performance improvement does this protocol predict, and under what conditions does it fail?
3. **Verify Against the Whole Body of Evidence:** Does this claim contradict 50+ years of human exercise physiology literature regarding volume, recovery, and cardiac remodeling?
4. **Prioritize the Macro Over the Marginal:** 95%+ of performance gains stem from consistent sub-threshold volume, progressive threshold overload, maximal VO2max intervals, proper fueling (carbohydrates), and restorative sleep.

## Common Pitfalls & Limitations

> [!WARNING]
> **The Fallacy of Theoretical Shortcuts:** There are no mathematical "secrets", string theory analogs, or quantum shortcuts to bypassing the unsexy, time-dependent process of cellular adaptation (mitochondrial biogenesis, angiogenesis, and eccentric ventricular hypertrophy).

1. **Confusing Mathematical Models for Physical Reality:** Treating metrics like Normalized Power (NP), TSS, or W' balance as absolute physiological truths rather than rough mathematical approximations.
2. **Chasing Novelty Over Consistency:** Abandoning foundational training blocks (base endurance, progressive threshold intervals) to chase complex, highly marketed training fads.
3. **Ignoring Athlete Feedback & Autoregulatory Cues:** Relying solely on software modeling while disregarding an athlete's physical exhaustion, elevated RPE, or declining mental readiness.

## Summary Checklist / Decision Table

| Concept | Physics / Mathematical Abstraction | Biological / Empirical Training Reality |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **System Behavior** | Deterministic, predictable via closed-form equations | Complex, adaptive, homeostatic, non-linear |
| **Optimization Target** | Exact mathematical values (e.g., perfect TSS curves) | Robust biological strain balanced with complete recovery |
| **Testing Validity** | Exact numerical derivation | Reproducible field performance & metabolic steady state |
| **Primary Driver** | Theoretical models & computational algorithms | Progressive mechanical overload, substrate availability, sleep |

> [!TIP]
> When evaluating training advice or software metrics, always remember: **If a training model cannot explain why an athlete fails or produces unrepeatable results in the real world, the model is flawed—not the athlete's physiology.**
