### **2\. A closed US coaching summit… and a live AI-coach demo**

I’ve just come back from a **closed coaching conference in the US** with many of America’s top coaches.

On stage, we did something I’ve been wanting to test in public:

  * We connected **Athletica’s new AI coach** to my own training history

  * The room asked it questions like they would to a human coach or sport scientist

  * In under a minute, it had **read my files, assessed my power profile, my recovery profile, recent sessions, and explained its reasoning** behind every response in clear language

In other words, it behaved like a **top-level sport science coach assistant** sitting on your laptop or phone, with perfect recall of your training history and our HIIT Science framework.

### 3\. What’s coming to you: New Athletica web interface, AI Coach, Athletica U

All of this is leading into a big step for Athletica in the new year:

  * **New Athletica Interface  
** A cleaner, faster **UI** designed around how athletes and coaches actually use the platform – not just how we’d like them to.

  * **Conversational AI Coach  
** Just like the US Olympic coaches mentioned, you’ll be able to ask questions like:

    * “How recovered do I appear today?”

    * “How have areas of my power profile changed in the last 6 weeks?”

    * “Should I adjust today’s session given how I’ve been sleeping?”  
And get answers grounded in **your data, your context, and our AI agents and algorithms** – not generic AI chat.
