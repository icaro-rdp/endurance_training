# The Pill That Made Exercise Work Less

For years, rapamycin has been hailed as the closest thing we have to a real longevity drug, taken by biohackers, prescribed by telehealth clinics, and promoted across the wellness internet.

The first proper randomised controlled trial combining it with exercise in older adults just dropped, and the results are a wake-up call about how we treat supplement hype.
