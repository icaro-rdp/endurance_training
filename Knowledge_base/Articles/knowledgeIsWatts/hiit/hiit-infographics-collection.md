LITERATURE BRIEFING:

Both in well-trained amateur and professional cyclists, short HIT intervals (3x13min alternating 30s
on and 15s off) seems to provide superior physiological and performance adaptations compared to Long
HIT intervals (4x5min with 2.5min rec.) when both formats are performed at the maximum sustainable
intensity. (1, 2)

When considering long HIT intervals, a fast start for each interval (1m30s at 100% VO2max power
followed by 3m30s at 85% of VO2max power) permits to spend more time at a high percentage of VO2max
(higher aerobic load) with a lower perceived effort compared to maintain a steady pace (all 5min at
90% of VO2max power). (3)

Ingesting sodium bicarbonate (0.4 g/kg) before HIT sessions for some weeks can boost training
adaptations and performance. (4)
