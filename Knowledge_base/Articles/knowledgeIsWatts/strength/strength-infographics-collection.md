LITERATURE BRIEFING:

In professional (continental level) cyclists, 2x heavy gym strength training sessions for 10 weeks
during the preparatory period improve W/kg performance in efforts lasting from 1s to 40min. So,
strength training can be beneficial for all rider types: sprinters, puncheurs, time trialists and
climbers. (1) 12 weeks of heavy gym strength training during the preparatory period improve
durability in welltrained amateur cyclists. (2)

Unilateral gym strength exercises seems to be more effective than bilateral ones to improve cycling
performance. (3)

Based on a bunch of studies, practical recommendations for implementing gym strength training to
optimize cycling performance can be found here. (4)
