# Books Summary Index

This folder contains high-density chapter summaries and structural indexes for the major reference books in `Knowledge_base/Books/`.

---

## 1. Training for the Uphill Athlete
- **File**: [`Knowledge_base/Books/Training for the Uphill Athlete.md`](<../Training for the Uphill Athlete.md>)
- **Authors**: Steve House, Scott Johnston, Kilian Jornet
- **Key Focus**: Aerobic capacity, physiology of endurance, mountain sports, strength training for uphill athletes, periodization.
- **Core Concepts**:
  - Aerobic Deficiency Syndrome (ADS): Identifying and correcting weak aerobic base.
  - Cardiac Hypertrophy: Eccentric stroke volume expansion vs concentric wall thickening.
  - Transition from Zone 2 base to muscular endurance and high-intensity interval blocks.

## 2. Training and Racing with a Power Meter
- **File**: [`Knowledge_base/Books/Training and Racing with a Power Meter.md`](<../Training and Racing with a Power Meter.md>)
- **Authors**: Hunter Allen, Andrew Coggan, Stephen McGregor
- **Key Focus**: Power-based training metrics, FTP, WKO metrics, power profile testing, workout design.
- **Core Concepts**:
  - 7 Power Training Zones (Active Recovery, Endurance, Tempo, Threshold, VO2max, Anaerobic Capacity, Neuromuscular Power).
  - Normalized Power (NP), Intensity Factor (IF), and Training Stress Score (TSS).
  - Power Profiling: 5s, 1min, 5min, 20min power to identify rider phenotype (Sprinter, Time Trialist, Climber, All-rounder).

## 3. Norwegian Singles Method Subthreshold
- **File**: [`Knowledge_base/Books/Norwegian Singles Method Subthreshold.md`](<../Norwegian Singles Method Subthreshold.md>)
- **Key Focus**: Controlled subthreshold lactate training, double-threshold days, high weekly volume with minimal autonomic stress.
- **Core Concepts**:
  - Keeping lactate strictly between 2.0 and 3.5 mmol/L during intervals to maximize lactate clearance capacity without excessive fatigue.
  - High frequency of quality subthreshold work (e.g. 5x6min, 4x8min at LT2/sub-LT2) paired with high volume Zone 2.

## 4. Injury-Free Running - Your Illustrated Guide
- **File**: [`Knowledge_base/Books/Injury-Free Running - Your Illustrated Guide.md`](<../Injury-Free Running - Your Illustrated Guide.md>)
- **Key Focus**: Running mechanics, cadence, tissue overload prevention, lower-limb strength and durability.
- **Core Concepts**:
  - Foot strike mechanics, Achilles tendon stiffness, calf/soleus strengthening, and progressive mileage loading.
