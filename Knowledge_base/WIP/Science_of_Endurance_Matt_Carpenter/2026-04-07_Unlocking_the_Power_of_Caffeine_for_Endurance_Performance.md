# Unlocking the Power of Caffeine for Endurance Performance

My video podcasts are now available on Spotify, Youtube and Apple Podcasts as well as Substack. Please do follow me there to support my weekly posts.

Find my Spotify platform [here](https://open.spotify.com/show/2khQ8MibuAWcXvxQZbhijJ?si=emIdfRZ_TpuTWNeEMQuQqA), Youtube [here](https://www.youtube.com/@Mattendurancescience) and Apple Podcasts [here](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/the-endurance-science-podcast-with-matt-carpenter/id1875561593).

In this episode, I dive deep into the science of caffeine, revealing how understanding its mechanisms, dosing, and timing can help endurance athletes maximise their performance safely and effectively.

**Main topics:**

  * The science behind how caffeine enhances endurance performance

  * Optimal caffeine dosing strategies (3-6 mg/kg) and the effect of individual differences

  * Timing of caffeine intake before and during races for maximum benefit

  * The history of caffeine’s status in anti-doping regulations and its legal use today

  * Differences between coffee and pure caffeine supplements

  * Practical tips for training and race day caffeine use

• The importance of personal experimentation and understanding individual responses
