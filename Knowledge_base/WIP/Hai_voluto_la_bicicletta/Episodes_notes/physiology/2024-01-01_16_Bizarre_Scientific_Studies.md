# 16: Bizarre and Unconventional Scientific Studies in Sports Physiology

## Overview and Scientific Context

During the holiday season, hosts Daniele Bazzana and Stefano Nardelli take an entertaining yet scientifically rigorous look into unusual, unconventional, and bizarre exercise physiology studies published in peer-reviewed scientific literature. 

Behind these seemingly strange experimental setups lie fundamental physiological questions regarding bioenergetics, autonomic control, maximal capacity, and training adaptation boundaries.

---

## 1. Study 1: The Critical Power and Bioenergetics of Racehorses

Can mathematical models designed for human cyclists be applied to equine physiology?
- **Equine Bioenergetics:** Thoroughbred racehorses possess extraordinary aerobic and anaerobic engines, exhibiting $VO_2max$ values exceeding **$160\text{ to } 180\text{ mL/kg/min}$** (more than double that of elite human cyclists).
- **Critical Velocity ($CV$) Testing on Treadmills:** Researchers placed horses on specialized high-speed treadmills with automated respiratory masks and blood lactate sampling catheters across multiple all-out sprint durations.
- **Scientific Findings:** The hyperbolic Power-Duration / Speed-Duration curve holds remarkably true across species. Racehorses exhibit distinct Critical Velocity ($CV$) thresholds and anaerobic work capacities ($W'$), demonstrating that the two-parameter hyperbolic bioenergetic model is a universal principle of mammalian muscular locomotion.

## 2. Study 2: Pre-Exercise Bowel Evacuation and Time to Exhaustion

Does going to the bathroom prior to an intense athletic event have a measurable physiological impact beyond mere weight reduction?
- **Experimental Design:** Cyclists completed maximal Time to Exhaustion ($TTE$) tests at their anaerobic threshold ($LT_2 / MLSS$) under two randomized conditions: after complete pre-exercise bowel evacuation vs. with an un-evacuated bowel.
- **Physiological Mechanisms:**
  - *Intra-Abdominal Pressure:* Rectal distension and full bowels increase intra-abdominal pressure and stimulate visceral mechanoreceptors.
  - *Autonomic & Parasympathetic Modulation:* Elevated visceral tension increases baseline discomfort and parasympathetic/sympathetic autonomic disturbance.
- **Results:** Athletes who evacuated prior to exercise demonstrated a **statistically significant increase in Time to Exhaustion at threshold** and lower Rate of Perceived Exertion ($RPE$) during submaximal stages, confirming that gastrointestinal comfort directly modulates central exercise tolerance.

## 3. Study 3: The Extreme Stimulus of 27 VO2max Ramp Tests Over 9 Weeks

What happens when an athlete performs maximal $VO_2max$ ramp tests three times a week for nine consecutive weeks?
- **Experimental Setup:** Untrained subjects were subjected to 3 incremental metabolimeter ramp tests to exhaustion per week for 9 weeks (a total of 27 maximal ramp tests) with no other training.
- **Physiological Outcomes:**
  - *Weeks 1–4 (Rapid Initial Surge):* $VO_2max$ increased rapidly by $15\text{--}20\%$ within the first 12 sessions, accompanied by marked reductions in submaximal heart rate.
  - *Weeks 5–9 (Plateau & Autonomic Fatigue):* Adaptations completely stagnated after week 4. Without aerobic low-intensity base volume ($Zone\,2$), the repeated maximal central stress triggered autonomic fatigue, elevated resting heart rate, and blunted further performance gains.
- **Takeaway:** Ramp testing serves as a potent high-intensity interval stimulus, but rapidly leads to diminishing returns and overreaching if not supported by balanced, polarized training intensity distribution.

---

## Key Takeaways and Practical Recommendations

- **Universal Bioenergetic Laws:** Mathematical models like Critical Power and $W'$ describe universal properties of oxidative and glycolytic metabolism across mammalian physiology.
- **Optimize Pre-Race Digestion:** Pre-race bowel emptying improves diaphragmatic excursion, reduces intra-abdominal pressure, and lowers subjective effort perception.
- **Avoid Monotonous Maximal Testing:** Testing is a severe physiological stressor; diagnostic testing should be scheduled strategically every 6–12 weeks rather than used as regular weekly workouts.
