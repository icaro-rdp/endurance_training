# 110: Recommended Workouts for Different Rider Phenotypes

## Overview and Phenotypic Profiling

In traditional cycling jargon, riders are often classified into rigid archetypes: *climbers*, *sprinters*, and *rouleurs*. However, modern exercise physiology classifies athletes based on their **bioenergetic phenotype**—the interaction between aerobic power ($	ext{VO}_2	ext{max}$), maximal glycolytic power ($	ext{VLamax}$), muscle fiber architecture (Type I slow-twitch vs. Type II fast-twitch distribution), and substrate kinetics.

Applying identical training sessions to different physiological phenotypes yields vastly different adaptations and fatigue profiles. A workout that provides an ideal aerobic stimulus for a diesel climber can induce severe glycogen depletion and autonomic exhaustion in a fast-twitch sprinter. This episode details specific, evidence-based workout structures optimized for three distinct rider profiles.

---

## 1. The Fast-Twitch / Glycolytic Phenotype (The Sprinter / Puncher)

### Physiological Profile:
- High percentage of **Type IIa and IIx** fast-twitch muscle fibers.
- High $	ext{VLamax}$ ($> 0.6 	ext{ mmol/L/s}$), producing massive anaerobic power and explosive sprint speed.
- High rate of glycolytic flux and rapid lactate accumulation at moderate workloads.
- *Limiter:* Accelerated glycogen depletion and lower fractional utilization at threshold ($pprox 70	ext{--}75\% 	ext{VO}_2	ext{max}$).

### Recommended Workouts:
1. **Low-Cadence Sub-Threshold / Torque Tempo:**
   - *Structure:* $3 	imes 12	ext{--}15 	ext{ min}$ at $80	ext{--}85\%$ FTP at $50	ext{--}60 	ext{ rpm}$.
   - *Physiological Goal:* Recruits fast-twitch motor units at a sub-maximal, oxidative metabolic rate, stimulating mitochondrial enzyme activity in Type II fibers without driving high glycolytic acid-base disturbance.
2. **Short Sprint Interval Training (SIT) with Full Recovery:**
   - *Structure:* $4	ext{--}6 	imes 15	ext{--}20 	ext{ s}$ maximal sprints with $4	ext{--}5 	ext{ min}$ passive/very easy recovery.
   - *Physiological Goal:* Maximizes neuromuscular motor unit recruitment and peak power output while allowing complete phosphocreatine (PCr) resynthesis between reps.
3. **Cautionary Guideline:** Avoid excessively long, continuous sweet-spot or threshold intervals ($>40 	ext{ min}$ time-in-zone per session), which trigger severe metabolic exhaustion and blunt peak sprint power.

## 2. The Slow-Twitch / Oxidative Phenotype (The Diesel / Climber)

### Physiological Profile:
- High percentage of **Type I** oxidative muscle fibers.
- Low $	ext{VLamax}$ ($< 0.35 	ext{ mmol/L/s}$) and high mitochondrial density.
- Exceptional fat oxidation capacity and high fractional utilization at threshold ($85	ext{--}90\% 	ext{VO}_2	ext{max}$).
- *Limiter:* Low anaerobic capacity ($W'$) and sluggish response to sudden, explosive race surges.

### Recommended Workouts:
1. **Extensive Threshold Repeats (TTE Expansion):**
   - *Structure:* $3 	imes 20 	ext{ min}$ or $2 	imes 30 	ext{ min}$ at $95	ext{--}100\%$ FTP with $5	ext{--}8 	ext{ min}$ active recovery.
   - *Physiological Goal:* Capitalizes on high oxidative endurance to push Time-to-Exhaustion (TTE) at threshold out to $50	ext{--}70 	ext{ min}$.
2. **Short Intermittent Microbursts (Rønnestad 30/15s):**
   - *Structure:* $3 	ext{ sets of } (10 	imes 30	ext{s at } 120\% 	ext{ FTP} / 15	ext{s recovery})$, $4 	ext{ min}$ between sets.
   - *Physiological Goal:* Stimulates fast-twitch fiber recruitment and pushes cardiac output near $	ext{VO}_2	ext{max}$ without generating unmanageable lactate accumulation, helping the diesel athlete develop punchiness.

## 3. The Balanced Phenotype (The All-Rounder / Rouleur)

### Physiological Profile:
- Moderate $	ext{VLamax}$ ($0.40	ext{--}0.50 	ext{ mmol/L/s}$) and solid $	ext{VO}_2	ext{max}$.
- Versatile energy system capable of both steady climbing and closing gaps.

### Recommended Workouts:
1. **Over-Under Lactate Clearance Intervals:**
   - *Structure:* $3 	imes 12 	ext{ min}$ ($2 	ext{ min at } 105\% 	ext{ FTP} / 2 	ext{ min at } 90\% 	ext{ FTP}$).
   - *Physiological Goal:* Trains MCT transporter dynamics, conditioning the athlete to handle and clear the repeated power surges characteristic of road racing.
2. **Macrocycle Periodization:** Periodize stimuli according to event demands—focusing on extensive aerobic base and threshold capacity for granfondos/stage races, or sharpening anaerobic repeatability for criteriums and rolling road races.

## Key Takeaways and Practical Recommendations

- **Profile Before Prescribing:** Identify the athlete's metabolic phenotype (glycolytic vs. oxidative) before selecting interval workouts.
- **Preserve Fast-Twitch Strengths:** Do not drown sprinters in excessive threshold volume; maintain low-cadence torque and full-recovery sprint drills to keep neuromuscular pathways sharp.
- **Sharpen Diesel Riders with Microbursts:** Use intermittent 30/15 or 40/20 intervals to give oxidative climbers the ability to respond to tactical attacks.
- **Train for the Goal Event:** While respecting baseline physiology, align the final 8–12 weeks of training with the specific metabolic demands of the target race.
