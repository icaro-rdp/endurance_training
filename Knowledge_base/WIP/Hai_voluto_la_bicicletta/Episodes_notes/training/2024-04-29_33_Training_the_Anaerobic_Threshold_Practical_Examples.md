# 33: Training the Anaerobic Threshold: Practical Examples

## Overview and Anaerobic Threshold Concepts

Following the physiological breakdown of metabolic thresholds in Episode 5, Daniele Bazzana and Stefano Nardelli focus on the **practical execution of Anaerobic Threshold (LT2 / MLSS / FTP) workouts**. The Anaerobic Threshold represents the highest power output or metabolic rate at which blood lactate production and clearance are in steady-state equilibrium.

This episode details the three primary interval categories used to elevate threshold power and extend Time to Exhaustion (TTE): **Intensive Threshold**, **Extensive Threshold (Sweet Spot)**, and **Over-Under clearance intervals**.

---

## 1. Intensive Threshold Intervals (Pushing the Ceiling)

### Structure & Parameters
- **Duration:** Intervals lasting 6 to 10 minutes (e.g., 4 x 8 min, 5 x 8 min, 3 x 10 min, 4 x 10 min).
- **Target Intensity:** 98–105% FTP / LT2 power.
- **Work-to-Rest Ratio:** 2:1 to 3:1 (e.g., 8 min work / 2–3 min active recovery).
- **Target Volume:** 30 to 45 minutes total Time-in-Zone.
- **Objective:** Maximizing the absolute wattage sustainable at maximal lactate steady state by challenging both oxidative phosphorylation and lactate buffering capacity.

---

## 2. Extensive Threshold & Sweet Spot Intervals (Extending TTE)

### Structure & Parameters
- **Duration:** Long sustained blocks lasting 15 to 30 minutes (e.g., 3 x 15 min, 2 x 20 min, 3 x 20 min, 2 x 30 min).
- **Target Intensity:** 88–95% FTP (Sweet Spot / Subthreshold).
- **Work-to-Rest Ratio:** 4:1 to 6:1 (e.g., 20 min work / 4–5 min easy spin).
- **Target Volume:** 45 to 75+ minutes total Time-in-Zone.
- **Objective:** Expanding **Time to Exhaustion (TTE)** at threshold, enhancing fat oxidation kinetics, increasing muscular mitochondrial enzyme density (citrate synthase), and building metabolic durability.

---

## 3. Over-Under Intervals (Lactate Clearance and Shuttling)

Over-Unders represent one of the most physiologically potent methods to train the **Brooks Lactate Shuttle** and upregulate monocarboxylate transporters (MCT1 and MCT4):

```
[ Over-Under Set: 12–18 Minutes Total ]
├─ "UNDER" Phase: 2–3 min @ 88–92% FTP (Aerobic clearance via MCT1 into Type I fibers)
└─ "OVER" Phase:  1–2 min @ 108–115% FTP (Glycolytic flux & lactate production via MCT4)
```

- **Mechanism:** During the "OVER" segment, fast-twitch motor units produce lactate and hydrogen ions ($H^+$). During the "UNDER" segment, power remains sufficiently high that the rider cannot rest, forcing oxidative slow-twitch muscle fibers and cardiac muscle to clear and oxidize lactate as a direct fuel substrate.

3. **Phase 3 (Lactate Dynamics):** 3 x 12 min Over-Unders (Conditioning clearance).
4. **Phase 4 (Durability):** 2 x 25 min or 1 x 45 min @ 92–95% FTP.

---

## Key Takeaways and Practical Recommendations

- **Extend Duration Before Raising Power:** Progress threshold workouts from 3 x 10 min to 2 x 20 min and 3 x 20 min before bumping your FTP setting.
- **Utilize Over-Unders for Clearance:** Implement alternating 2 min Under (90%) / 1 min Over (110%) blocks to train the intracellular lactate shuttle.
- **Use Sweet Spot for High Volume:** Accumulate 45–60 minutes of sweet spot work to stimulate mitochondrial density with lower systemic fatigue than all-out FTP sessions.
- **Match Recovery to Interval Length:** Keep rest intervals short (2–4 minutes) during threshold training to prevent complete metabolic reset.
