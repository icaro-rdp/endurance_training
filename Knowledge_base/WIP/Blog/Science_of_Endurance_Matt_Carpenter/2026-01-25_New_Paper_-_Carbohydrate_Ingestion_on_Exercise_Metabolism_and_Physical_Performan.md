# New Paper - Carbohydrate Ingestion on Exercise Metabolism and Physical Performance

<abstract lang="en">
This paper has being all over social media this week. I discuss the interpretation and my own thoughts on this thought provoking review.
</abstract>

This new publication by Noakes et al. (2026) has been all over social media, with some saying that the ‘carb loading myth is dead’. Here I run through some of the misinterpretations of this review, as well as my own thoughts on the key claims.

This just happens to be very closely related to my PhD thesis work, so it was good to see it come to many similar conclusions, though as I discuss in the video, there are areas that I differ in my thoughts - all discussed above.
