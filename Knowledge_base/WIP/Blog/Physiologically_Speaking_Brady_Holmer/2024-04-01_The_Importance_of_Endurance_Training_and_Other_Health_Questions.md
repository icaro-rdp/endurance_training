# The Importance of Endurance Training and Other Health Questions

<abstract lang="en">
I joined Aidan Chariton on his podcast Shortcut U to discuss endurance performance and other questions about health and fitness.
</abstract>

[Today, I’m sharing an interview that I did with ](<https://open.substack.com/pub/shortcutu/p/sup-003-why-endurance-training-is?r=6eiws&utm_campaign=post&utm_medium=web>) [for his Substack Shortcut U.](<https://open.substack.com/pub/shortcutu/p/sup-003-why-endurance-training-is?r=6eiws&utm_campaign=post&utm_medium=web>)

**We discuss endurance training, why it's important for health, and how to approach it.**

I also answer some questions like:

> _Do we really need to be doing endurance training if we're already lifting weights?_
> 
> _Is sauna actually useful for health and performance?_
> 
> _Are coffee and energy drinks negatively impacting your cardiovascular health?_
